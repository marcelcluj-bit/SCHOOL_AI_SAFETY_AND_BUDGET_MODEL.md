## 1. VISION & CORE MISSION

School violence, weapons, and sudden tragedies in educational institutions require real-time prevention rather than post-incident reactions. This repository provides a **fully open-source, zero-cost-license software framework** that transforms existing school security camera feeds into an autonomous AI-monitored safety grid using multimodal AI (Gemini Vision).

By detecting weapons (firearms, knives), illicit substances, and aggressive physical assaults within seconds, the system instantly alerts authorities and school security, preventing tragedies before they escalate.

## 2. MACRO BUDGET SCALE: SECURING THOUSANDS OF SCHOOLS AT 1-3% COST

Governments often assume that upgrading national school security requires massive capital expenditures. However, utilizing modular software, existing camera infrastructure, and edge-computing hardware, national deployments become exceptionally cost-effective.

### Financial Breakdown for a Medium School (approx. 500–800 students):
* **Existing Camera Integration:** Uses current IP cameras ($0 hardware replacement cost).
* **Edge Processing Unit (Mini-PC / AI Accelerator):** ~€1,000 – €1,500 per school.
* **Cloud API & Software Licensing (Open Source):** €0 (MIT License).
* **Total Initial Setup Cost per Medium School:** **~€2,000 – €3,000**.

### National Scalability Projection:
If a national government targets equipping **3,000 to 5,000 schools**:
* **Total Investment Required:** ~€6 Million to €15 Million.
* **Comparison with National Education Budgets:** This represents a mere **1% to 3%** of a typical annual national education infrastructure budget, easily absorbable through routine digital transformation funds or EU resilience grants.
